### 人物题：敬佩的成功运动员（Oliveira）

**题目：Describe a successful sportsperson you admire.**

人物：Oliveira（李培楠）；以下沿用此前整理的完整口语稿。

#### 完整英文口语稿

I'd like to talk about a professional esports player named Oliveira. He used to compete in StarCraft II tournaments, but he has since retired.

In 2023, he won one of the most prestigious StarCraft II tournaments in the world, called IEM Katowice, and became the first Chinese player to win the championship. It was a historic moment for Chinese esports.

Back then, StarCraft II wasn't particularly popular in China, and the professional training system wasn't very well developed. As a result, Chinese players weren't considered very competitive internationally, and hardly anyone expected a Chinese player to become a world champion.

As for his personality, I think he's a pretty easygoing and genuine person. He often streams on Douyu, which is basically the Chinese equivalent of Twitch. I remember that, early in his career, he once came across some negative comments online and actually burst into tears during a livestream. So I think he has a rather innocent and childlike personality.

What I admire most about him is his determination. Despite the difficult circumstances, he kept looking for opportunities to practise with international players and spent hours improving his skills every day.

In the end, all his hard work paid off. Against all odds, he managed to win the world championship. His story really inspires me because it shows that even when nobody believes in you, you should never give up.

#### 语法提示：has since retired

> He used to compete in StarCraft II tournaments, but he has since retired.
>
> 他过去参加《星际争霸 II》的比赛，但后来已经退役了。

- **has + retired（过去分词）** 构成现在完成时，强调过去发生的退役对现在的影响。
- **since** 在这里是副词，表示“此后、从那以后”，指从前文提到的时期到现在；后面不需要再接时间或从句。
- **used to + 动词原形** 描述过去的习惯或状态，与 **has since + 过去分词** 搭配可表达后来的变化。

## 旧笔记归档

### 旧 Part 1 答案

#### 人生阶段

- **What did you often do with your friends in your childhood?**

	We used to be really into Minecraft. Back then, we didn't know how to play together online, so we'd often go to each other's homes and connect to the same Wi-Fi to play together. As we got older, we got interested in other games and learned how to play Minecraft on servers, so we stopped doing that.

- **What do you think is the most important at the moment？**

	It's hard to say what matters most right now. I just want to keep improving myself so that when I discover something I'm truly passionate about, I'll be well prepared to pursue it.

- **Do you have any plans for the next five years？**

	Not exactly. Technology is developing so rapidly these days that I have no idea what the future will look like. I also believe that no one can predict exactly what will happen over the next five years.
	
	So I think it's better to focus on the coming months or the next year rather than plan everything five years ahead. Right now, I just want to keep improving myself and prepare for whatever the future holds（将来发生什么）.

- **How do people remember each stage of their lives？**

	I think it depends on the major changes people experience in their lives. For example, people may feel that they've entered a new stage of life when they graduate from high school or go to university.

	Besides, major achievements and setbacks can also mark（标记） different stages of someone's life. You can imagine life as a line graph. Sometimes it goes up, and sometimes it goes down. I think the turning points（转折点） on that graph are often what people remember most.

- **Do you enjoy being the age you are now?**

	I'd say yes, considering the competition and financial pressure I might face in the future. At least I still have plenty of free time to chill out for now（暂时如此）.

- **At what age do you think people are the happiest**

	Definitely childhood, probably between the ages of 6 and 12. At that age, children are usually taken care of and haven't had to deal with the pressure of intense competition yet. In their eyes, the world is usually full of innocence and beauty.

#### 风景与地点（View）

- **Do you like taking pictures of different views?** 

	Oh, definitely! I really enjoy looking at beautiful landscapes, and I often take photos of them. Sometimes I even take screenshots while playing games with picturesque scenery, such as Wuthering Waves and Forza Horizon 6.
	
- **Do you prefer views in urban areas or rural areas?** 

	Hard to say, perhaps rural areas. Since I live in a city, rural scenery feels quite novel to me. Also, I think photos of the city taken from the countryside often have a special feel to them（某个东西给人一种……的感觉）.

- **Do you prefer views in your country or in other countries?**

	I'm not a big fan of traveling, so I can't really make a fair comparison. I'd say I prefer China, although I've seen some photos of other countries on the internet.

#### 出行与景色（Scenery）

- **Scenery Do you look out the window at the scenery when travelling by bus or car?** 

	Yes. Since I spend most of my time indoors,  it is a pleasant and relaxing way to kill time（消磨时光） .

- **Do you prefer the mountains or the sea?** 

	I prefer the sea. I don't really like climbing mountains, and the sea always feels cool and relaxing. Admittedly, scenery by the sea doesn't change much with the seasons. In contrast, some mountain forests turn golden yellow in autumn, which creates a really special atmosphere（营造一种特别的氛围）.

- **Do you like to take scenery pictures?**（和之前的答案有很多相似之处）
  
	Of course. I'm particularly fond of picturesque landscapes. I even get to show off（展示，悬崖） my photography skills in games with picturesque scenery, such as Wuthering Waves and Forza Horizon 6. 
	
	But I don't usually go out of my way to （特意费心去做某事） look for the perfect scene. I prefer it when I just happen to come across（偶然遇见） a beautiful view, almost as if we were meant to meet（仿佛我们注定会相遇）.

-  **What are the most beautiful sights you have seen while travelling?**

	I don't travel very often, so this is a bit hard to answer. But I remember being in a taxi in a Chinese city on a rainy day, although I can't quite remember its name. The raindrops on the window made the lights outside look like a starry sky. It was beautiful and made me feel a little nostalgic because it reminded me of the only time I had seen a real starry sky, when I was a kid walking home with my grandma.
