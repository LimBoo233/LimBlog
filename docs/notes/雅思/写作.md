图表：
- graph（通用的图表），chart（通用，但表格不太合适）
	1. line chart 线图
	2. pie chart 饼图
	3. bar chart 柱状图
	4. table/diagram 表格
	5. process chart / flow chart 流程图
	6. map 地图
- the former / the latter

展现：
- present
- show
- illustrate
- compare（除了流程图）

时间跨度：
- 时间点
	- from ... to ...
	- between ... and ...
	- since ... until
- 时间段
	- over / through (the first / following / subsequent / next / last) 80 years （over 更常用语句首）
	- till / at the end of the period in question：最后一年（在问题中）
	- through a period of 80 years
	- through an 80-year period
	- period = span
	- 10 years = decade
- then on：自那之后


包含：
- namely
- i.e. (拉丁语起源)
- viz. （法语起源）
- abovementioned（上述的）

---

Overview

- Correspondingly：相应地（两表衔接，“似乎有关系”）


---

表述

- 百分比：percentage = proportion = share
- 大约：about = **approximately** = some = around -> ... roughly -> ... crudely
- 几乎：nearly = **virtually** = almost = **practically**
- 保持不动：keep unchanged = remain constant = maintain the same level = stabilize = plateau (v.)

> [!TIP]
> 其实不必要每次都用"大约"，即便与数据不符

- 增长：increase = rise = climb = **grow (growth)** = go up (upward trend)
	- 猛增：jump = soar = surge = spike = (sky)rocket
- 减少：decrease = decline = reduce (reduction) = drop = fall = go down (downward trend)
	- 猛减：dive（跳水）= plummet（坠落） = plunge
- 波动：
	- fluctuate => a fluctuate growth
	- fluctuation：主要是伴随
		- a growth with fluctuations：伴随波动的增长
		- with miner fluctuations：伴随轻微的波动
		- with occasional fluctuations：伴随偶尔的波动
		- despite some fluctuations：尽管有一些波动
	- 线图中，很快地下降再上升，类似 v 字，此动作称作 "dip(dipped) v. & n."
	- 最高顶：peak v. n.
		- peak at ...：达到...的峰值 
	- 最低点：bottom (out) v. n.
		- bottom out at...

- 表述程度的词（adj.）
	- slight：轻微的
	- mild, moderate, modest：温和的
	- steady：稳定的
	- **considerable, substantial, significant：较大的**
	- rapid, sharp, steep, **dramatic(lly)**：迅猛地'

> [!TIP]
> 有节制的用副词，不要滥用

特殊句式（所有线图题必用一次）

- The (first) 20 years **saw** an （considerable） increase in ... from 30% to 100%（时间做主语）
	- The (following) 2 decades **saw** an increase in share to 100%（不重复）

- A speedy decrease was shown / apparent
- 现在分词做状语：（一个增长）, peaking at ....
