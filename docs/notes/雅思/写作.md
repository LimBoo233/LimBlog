## 表格

将数据分组，分组依据可以有：
- 数据大小：最大的，其余相近的，然后最小的
- 类别：最后说 other ...

占据大小：
- occupy the (largest / highest) share of (42%) in / among ...

排名：
- 最大值
	- largest / highest
	- 排名第一，高居首位
		- rank the first among ...
		- top the list of ...
- 第二大值（离第一很近）
	- ... followed by ... , which (accounts for 30%)
	- closely follows, (accounting for ...)
	- with ... closely following (30%) <- *这个括号不是替换的意思，真就得这么写*
- 剩下的小数值（相对不重要）
	- respectively 一起写：A, B and C take up 5, 4, and 3 respectively.
	- 区间、范围：
		- range from ... to ...：The shares of A, B and C range from 3 and 5.
		- cluster between ... and ... ：The shares of A, B and C cluster between 3 and 5
		- "-" ：The shares of A, B and C account for 3-5 each.
- 相似的数字比较
	- 略小于
		- ... , slightly higher / larger than (that of B (14%) )
		- ..., a number slightly higher / larger than (that of B (14%) )
	- 两倍：... , twice as large as  (that of B (14%) )
	- 相等：A and B are equal at ... / A and B are match up at ... 
	- 可以用 respectively 来表述具体数字

> [!TIP]
> - with sth. doing 表述结果
> - 饼图还可以将多个数据加在一起： The shares of A, B and C combined account for 9.
